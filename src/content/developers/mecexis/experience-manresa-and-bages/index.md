Tourism platform created by Bàsquet Manresa, the city's professional basketball club, to connect match days with the Bages region. Visiting fans find partner restaurants, accommodation, wineries and cultural activities, a calendar of local events and match experiences they can book and pay for online. Available in Catalan, Spanish and English, with automatic translation through Wagtail AI.
