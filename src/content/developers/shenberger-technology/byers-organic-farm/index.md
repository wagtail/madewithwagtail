Byers Organic Dairy, LLC is a family farm with a vision to provide healthy food for the Mercersburg area and the surrounding Franklin County, Pennsylvania area. All of our animals are raised on acreage that has either never been sprayed with chemical weed killers or has not been sprayed in at least three years.  Our on-site store is open 24 hours a day except Sundays.
