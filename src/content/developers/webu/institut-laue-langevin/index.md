The Institut Laue-Langevin website is the institutional site of a world-leading neutron science research centre that gives researchers from around the world access to its instruments. It presents the institute’s services and scientific instruments for prospective users, provides practical information for researchers already on site, and showcases the science produced there through news and events. Built on Wagtail and available in English and French, it uses a library of reusable components so the institute’s many editors can publish consistent, up-to-date content.
