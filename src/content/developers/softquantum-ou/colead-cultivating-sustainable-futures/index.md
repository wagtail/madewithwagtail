COLEAD (COmmittee Linking for Entrepreneurship, Agriculture and Development) works towards the sustainable transformation of food value chains, particularly horticultural ones, in the countries and regions where development needs are greatest.

Founded in 1973, this international not-for-profit association brings together farmers, businesses, professional organisations, technical partners and public institutions around one conviction: the economic viability of food value chains is the essential condition for their social and environmental sustainability.
