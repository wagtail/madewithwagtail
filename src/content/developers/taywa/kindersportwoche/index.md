Kindersportwoche offers sports holiday programmes for children, making it easy for families to discover activities and book their participation online.

Taywa developed a custom, fully automated booking and invoicing system using Django, handling registrations, payments, and administrative workflows. Wagtail CMS powers the editorial content, allowing the team to manage programmes, information, and website updates independently.

A simple, functional design keeps the experience intuitive and accessible, while the automated system significantly reduces administrative work for the organizers.
