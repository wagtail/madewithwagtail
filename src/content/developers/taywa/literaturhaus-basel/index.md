Literaturhaus Basel is a cultural meeting place for literature, hosting readings, conversations, and encounters with writers from around the world.

Taywa developed the website with Django and Wagtail, bringing Sonja Studer’s distinctive design to life on the web. Bold typography, clear navigation, and a strong focus on the event programme create an engaging digital experience.

Wagtail enables the Literaturhaus team to manage editorial content independently and efficiently.
