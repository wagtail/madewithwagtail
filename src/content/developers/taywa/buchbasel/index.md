BuchBasel is Switzerland’s largest curated international literature festival, bringing together authors, thinkers, and literature enthusiasts.

Developed by Taywa using Wagtail CMS and Django, the website offers an intuitive way to explore the festival programme, discover authors, browse events, and save personal favourites.

Working closely with an external designer, Taywa translated a distinctive visual identity into a responsive, accessible, and web-optimized experience that preserves the character of the festival.

Wagtail makes managing events, author profiles, and editorial content simple and efficient.
