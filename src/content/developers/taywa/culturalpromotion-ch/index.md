Culturalpromotion.ch helps artists and cultural professionals across Switzerland find suitable funding opportunities. Supported by the Federal Office of Culture and the Migros Culture Percentage, the platform provides a searchable directory of public and private funding institutions.

Taywa developed the platform using Django for structured data and Wagtail CMS for editorial content. This combination allows complex funding information to be managed efficiently while keeping editorial publishing simple and flexible.

Search and filtering by cultural discipline and region make it easy to find relevant funding organisations.
